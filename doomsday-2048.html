How Weapon Upgrades Work

  • 2× 9mm Pistol Ammo (Tier 1) merge into 7.62mm Rifle Ammo (Tier 2).
  • 2× Rifle Ammo (Tier 2) merge into 5.56mm Armor-Piercing (Tier 3).
  • 2× Stripper Clips (Tier 4) craft the Bio-Green Service Pistol (Tier 5)!
  • Keep forging through SMGs, Carbines, and PDWs to unlock Heavy Gatling Miniguns.
  • Victory Target: Combine two Vulcan Miniguns (Tier 10) to forge the Tier 11 Dual Vulcan Minigun (2048) and the Tier 12 Triple Gatling Overlord!

Swipe with mouse/touch or use Arrow Keys / WASD to merge weapons!
